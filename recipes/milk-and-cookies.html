Milk and Cookies

By: Me

Sometimes a bag of cookies is all you need.


Ingredients

  • 4 or more Oreo cookies (preferibly original or double stuf)
  • 1 glass of preferred milk prodcut

Steps

  1. Stack cookies into a single column

  2. Operating with one cookie at a time, dunk the cookie into the milk and hold for approx. 4 seconds.

  3. Remove cookie from milk, shove into mouth, take a sip of milk, and repeat the process until all cookies are consumed.

    4. If too many cookie remain with not enought milk, pour some more and keep going.
