Scrambled Eggs Anytime!

By: Me

Did you just get your wisdom tooth pulled and some gum scaling done? This is a meal you can chew with one side of your mouth without moving your jaw too much.


Ingredients

  • Vegetable or canola oil
  • 3 eggs
  • Optional scallions for serving

Steps

  1. Heat a small skillet on high heat. Meanwhile, whisk the eggs in a small bowl. Once the skillet is very hot, add 2 tablespoons of oil and swirl around the pan.

  2. Drop those eggs right into the skilllet and let them sizzle for 10 seconds or so. If they are flapping violently, turn the heat down a bit.

  3. Use a spatula to getly move the eggs around the pan, going for less of a scrambly mess and more for some piled eggs. Turn the heat off at this point and cook until you lose the snotty tectture.

  4. Throw the scallions on and maybe some soy sauce and seasame oil. Serve over hot rice.
