Scrambled Eggs Anytime!

By: Me

Ingredients

  • Vegetable or canola oil
  • 3 eggs
  • Optional scallions for serving

Steps

  1. Heat a small skillet on high heat. Meanwhile, whisk the eggs in a small bowl. Once the skillet is very hot, add 2 tablespoons of oil and swirl around the pan.
